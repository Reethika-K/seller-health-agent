# FIN-01: Payout delay rate rising

Category: Finance
Metrics: payout_delay_rate, refund_rate, avg_response_hours

**Symptom.** payout_delay_rate rises: more of the seller's settled orders are paid out later than the promised date.

**Likely causes.**
1. Account or document problems: bank details mismatch, pending KYC or tax documents, invoice errors.
2. Payment holds linked to disputes, returns or a rise in refunds.
3. Settlement problems on the platform side, usually affecting many sellers at the same time.
4. Reconciliation mismatches between the seller's records and the platform's.

**How to tell them apart.** If refund_rate rises together with payout_delay_rate, holds due to refunds and disputes are likely (cause 2). If only payout_delay_rate moves and the seller's other categories are stable, suspect documents or bank details (cause 1). If many sellers show the same jump in the same week, it is a platform-side issue (cause 3) and the seller needs no change.

**Knock-on effects.** Delayed payouts strain the seller's cash flow. This can show up later as slower restocking and more contact with seller support.

**First actions.** Check the status of KYC and bank details, list the held settlements and the hold reason, and raise a reconciliation request for mismatches.
