# CX-02: Complaint count rising

Category: Customer Experience
Metrics: complaint_count, late_shipment_rate, return_rate, avg_rating

**Symptom.** complaint_count (buyer complaints raised against the seller) increases compared with the seller's own baseline.

**Likely causes.**
1. Delivery problems: late or missing orders (look at late_shipment_rate).
2. Product problems: damaged, wrong or defective items (look at return_rate and avg_rating).
3. Service problems: unresolved queries, rude or slow replies (look at avg_response_hours and sla_compliance).
4. Policy issues: disputes about refunds or return conditions.

**How to tell them apart.** Complaints rising together with late_shipment_rate point to Logistics. Complaints rising with return_rate and falling avg_rating, while operations are stable, point to product or fulfilment quality. Complaints rising with slow responses and no change in returns point to Seller support. When complaint counts are small (single digits per day), judge the trend over a week rather than a single day.

**Knock-on effects.** A complaint-heavy period lowers ratings and increases returns. If complaints are not answered, the same buyers escalate, which loads seller support further.

**First actions.** Group the last two weeks of complaints by reason, answer the oldest open ones first, and fix the largest reason group.
