# CX-01: Ratings falling with returns rising

Category: Customer Experience
Metrics: avg_rating, return_rate, complaint_count, listing_quality_score, late_shipment_rate

**Symptom.** avg_rating falls clearly (a drop of one point or more is severe) while return_rate rises.

**Likely causes.**
1. Product quality problem: a defective batch, a changed supplier or a cheaper substitute.
2. Wrong or damaged items shipped: picking errors, poor packaging.
3. The listing promises something the product does not deliver (size, color, material).
4. Delivery experience is poor: late arrival drives low ratings and returns.

**How to tell them apart.** Check the other categories first. If listing_quality_score or content_flag_count worsened, the listing is the cause and the problem started in Marketing (cause 3). If late_shipment_rate rose, delivery is the cause and the problem started in Logistics (cause 4). If all operational and listing metrics are stable and complaint_count rises sharply, the product or the packing itself is the issue and Customer Experience is the true primary category (causes 1 and 2).

**Knock-on effects.** Complaints rise, response times can stretch as the seller handles disputes, and a lower rating reduces visibility and order_volume.

**First actions.** Read the return reasons for the last two weeks, group them (defective, wrong item, not as described), and quarantine suspect stock.
