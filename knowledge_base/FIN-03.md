# FIN-03: Finance problems showing up as support load

Category: Finance
Metrics: payout_delay_rate, refund_rate, avg_response_hours, sla_compliance

**Symptom.** A seller is flagged for Finance, and a few weeks later Seller support is also flagged (slower responses, lower sla_compliance).

**Why it happens.** Delayed payouts and unexplained refunds make sellers and buyers contact support more often. The seller's team spends time chasing settlements and replying to disputes, and has less time for routine buyer messages, so avg_response_hours rises and sla_compliance falls.

**How to recognize it.** The Finance flag came first, the Seller support flag started later, and no Logistics or Customer Experience problem explains the slower replies. If the response-time problem began first, with no payout or refund change, the cause is support capacity and this document does not apply.

**What it means for the action.** Improving response times alone will not fix the load. The payout or refund problem has to be resolved, or the contacts will continue. In the report, Finance should be the primary category and Seller support the secondary one.

**First actions.** Resolve the oldest held settlements, publish a clear status for disputed refunds, and handle the backlog of related messages.
