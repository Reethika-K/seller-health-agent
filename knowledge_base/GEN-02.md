# GEN-02: Temporary dip versus real decline

Category: General
Metrics: all metrics

**Symptom.** A category is flagged in one weekly run, and the question is whether it matters.

**Temporary dip.** Healthy sellers have short disruptions: a sale-day rush, a holiday, a courier strike, a short stock gap, one bad day of reviews. These last about a week and recover to the seller's own baseline in the next run.

**Real decline.** A real decline keeps getting worse or stays flagged in consecutive weekly runs. It often spreads to a second category after a week or two.

**How to tell them apart.**
1. Persistence: flagged in two consecutive weekly runs means treat as real. Flagged in one run only means watch.
2. Direction: the score in the second week is worse or the same, not recovering.
3. Spread: other categories start to worsen. A dip normally stays in one category.
4. Cause: a known short event (holiday, sale, strike) explains the dip.

**What it means for the action.** Do not escalate a one-week flag. Keep the seller on the watch list and check again next week. Escalate when the flag repeats.

**First actions.** For a first-time flag, record it and re-check on the next run. For a repeated flag, start the diagnosis for the primary category.
