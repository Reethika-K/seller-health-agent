# MKT-01: Listing quality score falling

Category: Marketing
Metrics: listing_quality_score, content_flag_count, order_volume, return_rate

**Symptom.** listing_quality_score drops well below the seller's own recent level, usually together with a fall in order_volume.

**Likely causes.**
1. Catalog content degraded: images, titles or attributes were removed or replaced with poorer ones, often after a bulk re-upload or a change of catalog team.
2. Listings were edited to meet a new template and lost required attributes (size, material, brand).
3. New listings were created by copying content from other products, so the content no longer matches the item.

**How to tell them apart.** If content_flag_count rises at the same time, the problem is content accuracy (causes 1 and 3). If the score falls without new flags, look for missing images or attributes (cause 2). If return_rate rises and avg_rating falls a week or two later, the listings no longer match the product (cause 3).

**Knock-on effects.** Lower search visibility reduces order_volume, often by a quarter or more. Mismatched listings later push return_rate up and avg_rating down, which moves the problem into Customer Experience.

**First actions.** Audit the top listings by order volume, restore images and required attributes, and stop bulk uploads until the template is checked.
