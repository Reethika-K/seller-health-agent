# MKT-02: Content accuracy flags rising

Category: Marketing
Metrics: content_flag_count, listing_quality_score, return_rate

**Symptom.** content_flag_count rises: more listings are flagged for inaccurate or non-compliant content on each day.

**Likely causes.**
1. Claims that cannot be backed up (wrong size, color, quantity, authenticity or ingredient claims).
2. Prohibited or restricted wording and images, for example unverified performance claims or misleading before-and-after photos.
3. Variant mix-ups, where one listing shows photos or details of a different variant.
4. A platform policy update that the seller's existing listings do not meet.

**How to tell them apart.** If listing_quality_score falls along with the flags, the content itself has degraded (causes 1 to 3). If flags rise but the score stays flat and the rise is sudden, a policy update is more likely (cause 4). Flags concentrated in recently created listings point to a new template or a bulk upload.

**Knock-on effects.** Flagged listings can be suppressed, which lowers order_volume. Buyers who receive items that do not match the description raise return_rate and lower avg_rating after a delay.

**First actions.** Review the flagged listings, correct claims and images, and re-submit them. Check the latest policy notice before editing everything.
