# GEN-01: Sudden versus gradual declines

Category: General
Metrics: all metrics

**Symptom.** A category crosses the flag threshold. The question is how quickly it got there.

**Sudden decline (within about a week).** Sudden changes usually have a trigger: a bulk upload, a courier switch, a staff change, a system or settings change, an account hold, or a supplier failure. The right first question is "what changed around that date?". The cause is often a single event and can be reversed.

**Gradual decline (over three to four weeks).** Gradual erosion usually means capacity or quality is drifting: dispatch capacity not keeping up with growth, content quality slipping across many listings, stock control weakening, a support team stretched thin. The right first question is "what has been slowly changing?". It usually takes a program of fixes and not a single reversal.

**How to tell which one it is.** Look at the weekly scores for the seller. A category that drops from healthy to critical within one or two weekly runs is sudden. A category that moves through Watch and At-risk over several runs is gradual.

**What it means for the action.** For sudden declines, check change logs and recent events around the start date. For gradual declines, review capacity and trends over the last two months.

**First actions.** Find the date the first metric moved, then check what changed on that date.
