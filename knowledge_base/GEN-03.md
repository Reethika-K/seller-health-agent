# GEN-03: How declines spread between categories

Category: General
Metrics: all categories

**Symptom.** A seller has two flagged categories. Which one is the root cause and which one is the consequence?

**Common knock-on paths.**
- Marketing problems (listing quality, flags, ad return) lead to Customer Experience problems: the product does not match the listing, so returns rise and ratings fall.
- Logistics problems lead to Seller support and Customer Experience problems: late and cancelled orders create complaints and more buyer messages.
- Inventory problems lead to Logistics problems: missing stock causes cancellations and late shipments.
- Customer Experience problems lead to Seller support problems: more complaints and disputes to answer.
- Finance problems lead to Seller support problems: payout and refund queries increase contacts.
- Seller support problems lead to Customer Experience problems: slow replies make buyers unhappier.

**How to decide the root cause.** The root cause is the category that was flagged first, or flagged for the most consecutive weeks. The consequence begins one to three weeks later. If both started in the same week, check which metrics fell first within the week.

**What it means for the action.** Report the earliest category as primary and the later one as secondary, and fix the primary one first. Fixing only the secondary category rarely holds.

**First actions.** Compare the first flagged week of each category and list them in order.
