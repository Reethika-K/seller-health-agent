# CX-03: Negative review rate rising

Category: Customer Experience
Metrics: negative_review_rate, avg_rating, late_shipment_rate, listing_quality_score

**Symptom.** negative_review_rate (the share of reviews rated 1 or 2 stars) climbs while the total number of reviews is similar.

**Likely causes.**
1. A specific product or batch got worse and most low ratings concentrate on it.
2. Delivery experience dropped, so buyers rate the service and not the product.
3. The listing sets expectations the product does not meet.
4. A short burst of unusual reviews (competitor activity or a dispute) that does not reflect real orders.

**How to tell them apart.** If low ratings concentrate on a few listings, the product is at fault. If they are spread across all listings and late_shipment_rate rose, the service is at fault. If listing_quality_score fell, check for listing mismatch. A one- or two-day burst that vanishes the following week is more likely cause 4 and should not be treated as a trend until it lasts two weekly runs.

**Knock-on effects.** avg_rating falls, which reduces conversion and order_volume. Negative reviews are also visible to future buyers for a long time.

**First actions.** Break the low reviews down by listing and by date, respond publicly to the main complaints, and fix the leading cause before asking for new reviews.
