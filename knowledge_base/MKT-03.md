# MKT-03: Ad return falling

Category: Marketing
Metrics: ad_return, listing_quality_score, order_volume

**Symptom.** ad_return (revenue earned per unit of ad spend) falls below the seller's own baseline while spend is unchanged.

**Likely causes.**
1. The advertised listings got worse: lower listing quality converts fewer clicks into orders.
2. Budget is spread across low-converting listings or broad keywords.
3. Price is no longer competitive, so clicks arrive but do not convert.
4. Increased competitor bidding in the category, especially around sale periods.

**How to tell them apart.** If listing_quality_score fell together with ad_return, fix the listings first; spending more will not help (cause 1). If ad_return fell but listing_quality_score is stable and order_volume fell too, look at bidding, keywords and price (causes 2 to 4). A drop that appears for many sellers in the same week points to competition or seasonality rather than this seller.

**Knock-on effects.** Poor ad return lowers order_volume. Sellers who keep paying for non-converting ads lose margin.

**First actions.** Pause the worst-performing ads, move the budget to the best listings by conversion, and review price against comparable products.
